about:
- Shape a research agenda around a real planning or design problem
- Develop methods and tools, and validate them empirically
- Work with real-world data, often alongside partner feedback
- Publish in high-quality journals and conferences
- Contribute to lab infrastructure when relevant (datasets, benchmarks, tools)
- Fully funded, project-based position

look:
- Urban AI and machine learning for design and planning decision support
- Human–AI interaction for design tools
- Spatial analytics, simulation, and benchmarking
- Applied research with public and private partners
